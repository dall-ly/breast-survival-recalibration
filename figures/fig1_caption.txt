Fig. 1. Study workflow. (a) Fixed clinical, gene and pathway risks are evaluated using 300 paired cohort allocations. Calibration U contains the first m patients; evaluation V contains the remaining patients or, in the post hoc common-evaluation analysis, the suffix after the largest budget. Four fixed strategies and exploratory selection are evaluated separately. Δ averages three predictor-specific Brier differences versus unchanged. (b) At main budgets, calibration-only selection pools OOB patient–replicate records before paired record resampling. Hexagons denote processes; cylinders denote data, following Davis et al. [11].
